Sheng-Bin Duan

I'm currently a Ph.D. student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Beijing Normal University , majoring in Intelligent Science and Technology.

Before that, I was jointly trained at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences , where I conducted research on brain-computer interfaces 🧠 and intelligent human-machine interaction under the supervision of Prof. Jian-Long Hao, Prof. Xiao-Hu Zhou and Prof. Zeng-Guang Hou. I received my M.S. degree from Shanxi University of Finance and Economics in 2026.

My research interests include brain-computer interfaces, multimodal learning, and embodied intelligence, with a focus on EEG decoding, vision-language-action models, and intelligent human-machine interaction.

Research

My research interests include brain-computer interfaces (BCIs), EEG decoding, online learning, time-series analysis, multimodal learning, and vision-language-action (VLA) models. I am particularly interested in adaptive BCI systems and intelligent human-machine interaction for healthcare, rehabilitation, and robotic control.
